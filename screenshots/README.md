## For App Store Connect (Mac)

1440×900 is an accepted Mac screenshot size. Upload in this order. The first screenshot is the one shown first on the listing. No captions or device frames are baked in. The shots are the app itself, full-bleed.

Photos, videos and audio in these shots are real Wikimedia Commons media, not generated images. Shot 01 is the product's own first-run board, so it uses the welcome assets. Shots 02–05 use none of those assets, and no asset repeats between shots 02–05: each photograph, video and audio file appears in exactly one of those four shots.

To recapture: resize the window to 1440x900, open a board in Bowerbird and take a screenshot of the app window (on macOS, Cmd+Shift+4, then Space, then click the window). On Linux, `BOWERBIRD_WINDOW_SIZE=1440x900` forces the window to that size for a full-bleed shot, since the default window is 800x600.
