## For App Store Connect (Mac)

1440×900 is an accepted Mac screenshot size. Upload in this order —
the first screenshot is the one shown first on the listing. No
captions or device frames are baked in; the shots are the app
itself, full-bleed.

Photos in these shots are real Wikimedia Commons photographs
(bowerbird, Kyoto garden, brutalist building), not generated images.

If you recapture on a Mac, the layout is the same and the text
renders in SF Pro natively. To recapture: resize the window to
1440x900, open a board in Bowerbird and take a screenshot of the
app window (on macOS, Cmd+Shift+4, then Space, then click the
window). On Linux, `BOWERBIRD_WINDOW_SIZE=1440x900` forces the
window to that size for a full-bleed shot, since the default
window is one third of the screen.
